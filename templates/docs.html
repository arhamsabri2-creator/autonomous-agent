FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I get better results?
Be specific with your goals. Instead of "AI news", try "latest AI news about large language models today". The more specific, the better the results.
How do I use deep research?
Add "deep research" or "comprehensive" to your goal. Example: "deep research on quantum computing applications in 2026".
How does memory work?
Tell the agent to "remember" something. Next time you ask a related question, it will use that memory to give you personalized results.
How do I personalize my daily digest?
Go to Settings and enter your interests separated by commas. Example: "AI, cricket, stocks, India tech". You'll get a personalized briefing at 6pm IST.
What is the run limit?
Free users get 3 runs per day. Pro users get unlimited runs. Upgrade from the dashboard.
